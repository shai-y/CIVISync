Evacuation Information

Information regarding designated evacuation facilities.

Designated Evacuation Centers

Contact your barangay or the Batangas City CDRRMO at 702-3902 to confirm which evacuation center is open and where your barangay’s pick-up point is. Batangas City’s ordinance assigns pick-up points and designated centers through barangay contingency plans.

Preparedness Information

Pack a Go Bag with drinking water and ready-to-eat food for three days, essential medicines, a first-aid kit, a flashlight, a whistle, a power bank, and important documents in a waterproof pouch.

ABOUT CIVISync

A more organized approach to community assistance.

CIVISync is a municipal citizen assistance and disaster response queue management system designed to centralize disaster-related assistance requests.

Through organized request management, manual priority assessment, and request tracking, the platform aims to support better coordination between residents and authorized municipal personnel.
