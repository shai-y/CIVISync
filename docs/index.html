ABOUT CIVISYNC

A more organized approach to community assistance.

CIVISync is a municipal citizen assistance and disaster response queue management system designed to centralize disaster-related assistance requests.

Through organized request management, manual priority assessment, and request tracking, the platform aims to support better coordination between residents and authorized municipal personnel.
